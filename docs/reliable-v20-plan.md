# v20: prediction error, calibrated intervention, and repeated decisions

Scope approved 2026-09-09: repair the v19 advice reliability gaps, without expanding the market. v19 code, response model, source experiments and release archives remain frozen. v19's 720 states are development/regression data, never new holdout evidence.

1. Recompute intervention by economic action inequality, separately for profit and welfare; report coverage, conditional loss rate, loss magnitude, lower-tail mean, and seed-clustered uncertainty.
2. Offline diagnosis: fixed v19 selected action versus its exact draft. A public reconstructed state/current response, B true state/current response, C public state/actual stateful policy, D true state/actual stateful policy. Copy policy memory independently in every branch. D must exactly reproduce the original evaluation. Use common independent future random paths across A–D; keep policy identities fixed. Include all 28 negatives and matched nonnegative interventions. Truth and oracle policies must never enter production.
3. Select repairs from diagnosis. Fit an empirical overprediction budget on newly selected recommendations, using calibration seeds 610001–610030. Freeze before holdout seeds 620001–620030. Each seed covers N=2/5/10 and profit/welfare; rotate six market regimes deterministically to cover every cell. This is 180 states per split, 30 seed clusters; per-cell results remain exploratory. Unsupported modes/objectives/configurations and observations outside calibration support abstain explicitly. Never claim a distribution-free safety guarantee.
4. Compare v19 and repaired advice, including matched coverage and all-case gain with abstention zero. Do not choose thresholds on holdout. Negative-rate point estimates alone cannot establish low risk; report independent units and intervals. Profit and welfare have separate release gates.
5. Rolling evaluation: A fresh rule draft every round, B advice only first round then fresh rule, C fresh draft plus advice every round. A single focal company adopts, opponents update branch-specific memories normally. New seeds 630001–630010, N=2/5/10 and both goals, six rounds. Preserve per-round proposals, interventions, support checks and realized accounting.
6. Inspect existing corrected cooperation results first. Paid LLM experiments retain the original gate and 30 CNY cumulative authorization; no bypass or new calls unless prerequisites pass. Engineering completion and failed economic acceptance must be reported separately.

Every run records inputs, source hashes, seeds, actual actions and result hashes. Market accounting is unchanged. No tuning against holdout or erasure of negative evidence.

Before holdout: snapshot burn-in rotates 4–9 rounds so calibration covers decision rounds 5–10; each branch retains at least five future rounds. Default quantile is 0.90, minimum adjusted gain is zero, and calibration requires at least 12 selected cases from 8 distinct seeds per goal. Compare equal counts by ordering on model-only margins, never realized outcomes. Release acceptance requires positive all-case mean gain lower bound, coverage >=10% (profit) or >=5% (welfare), and a conservative one-sided 95% upper bound <=10% on the fraction of intervention-bearing seed clusters with any loss. This last measure is deliberately stricter than individual-intervention loss probability. Rolling evaluation separately requires positive paired mean lower bound for every-round versus no advice and reports every-round versus once; 10 rolling seed clusters cannot establish rare-loss reliability.
